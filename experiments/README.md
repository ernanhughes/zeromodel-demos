# Experiments

Experiments are bounded investigations of new ZeroModel ideas, representations, interfaces, and failure modes.

This is the least stable area of the repository. An experiment may succeed, fail, expose an observability boundary, or show that a proposed representation is inadequate. Negative results are valuable when they are documented honestly.

## Required discipline

Every experiment should state:

- the hypothesis;
- the permitted inputs and observations;
- the candidate representation or mechanism;
- the evaluation method;
- the result;
- known confounders;
- what would falsify the conclusion;
- whether the result should be promoted, repeated, or archived.

## Promotion

An experiment may move toward:

```text
experiment → lab
experiment → demo
```

only after it has reproducible instructions and a stable interpretation.

## Non-goals

Experiments must not be presented as released ZeroModel capabilities merely because code exists. Website visibility should remain disabled until an explicit promotion review occurs.
