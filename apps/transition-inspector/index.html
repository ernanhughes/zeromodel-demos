          Application context

          Why this matters

          Operational teams often need more than a screenshot diff. They need to know what the active policy expected, what actually happened, which declared components explain the result, and whether missing evidence was absent at source or lost during representation.

          Representative applications

          • autonomous-system incident review;
          • industrial or robotic transition verification;
          • game and simulation testing;
          • UI regression testing.

          What ZeroModel contributes

          ZeroModel contributes explicit policy expectation, visual transition evidence, component-level attribution, evidence-boundary classification, immutable identifiers, and reproducibility metadata.

          These are representative application patterns, not claims of current production deployment or validated performance in these industries.
