# Initial Demo Roadmap

This roadmap turns the current ZeroModel research sequence into a public product journey.

## Phase 1 — Understand ZeroModel

### Visual Policy Map Lookup

Class: `demo`

Show structured state being compiled into an addressable visual artifact, followed by deterministic lookup without a runtime model call.

### Compiled Intelligence Overview

Class: `showcase`

A short visual experience explaining the difference between generated intelligence and compiled, inspectable intelligence.

## Phase 2 — See the evidence

### Transition Evidence

Class: `demo`

Show before/after frames, declared component regions, extracted evidence, and transition conformance.

### Visual Evidence Inspector

Class: `lab`

Allow users to inspect regions, decoded values, changed fractions, signed changes, and conformance results.

## Phase 3 — See representation matter

### Representation Failure Explorer

Class: `lab`

Compare coarse and fine geometry, mean and max aggregation, and the information lost by each choice.

### Value-Aware Contracts

Class: `demo`

Demonstrate that component presence alone can hide direction, magnitude, and numeric-state failures.

## Phase 4 — See the compiler

### Evidence Contract Compiler

Class: `showcase`

Present bounded candidate generation, development-only selection, held-out evaluation, and parity with the historical manual representation in recoverable cases.

## Phase 5 — See the epistemic boundary

### Observability Boundary

Class: `showcase`

Contrast visible warehouse identity evidence with arcade target identity that is absent from the permitted observation.

The key message:

> ZeroModel can distinguish a representation that lost evidence from an observation that never contained it.

## Launch priority

Recommended first public sequence:

1. Visual Policy Map Lookup
2. Transition Evidence
3. Representation Failure Explorer
4. Evidence Contract Compiler
5. Observability Boundary

This sequence moves from immediate comprehension to the strongest research claim without requiring visitors to understand the full internal architecture first.
